One image summing up the world's news of Friday, 20 November 2026: Russia's
largest strike of the month on Kyiv (with missiles landing in Poland), the
AI sell-off that pushed Wall Street into correction, Cyclone Akvan's landfall
in Odisha, a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Peru, Trump's White House press
conference a day after the Shenzhen summit, a tanker struck in the Strait of
Hormuz, and the COP31 endgame in Antalya — plus three more stories in the
"Also today" strip. Every fact on the image was checked against reporting
from Reuters, AP, the BBC, ISW, the India Meteorological Department and
UKMTO; see work/research-20261120.md for the sources and the stories that
were left out because they happened on 19 November, not the 20th.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (drawn by `build_image.py`
with Pillow and the system DejaVu fonts) to the output directory, so both
graded runs write identical bytes.
